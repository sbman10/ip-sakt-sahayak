# Case Law Documentation Policy

## 1. Authoritative Requirements
To prevent hallucinations and speculative legal advice, case-law records cannot be promoted to active knowledge sources without:
1. **Certified Docket / Citation:** Neutral citation, official reporter citation (e.g., SCC, AIR, PTC), or High Court Writ Petition number.
2. **Court / Tribunal Identifier:** Supreme Court of India, specific High Court and Bench, or designated Commercial Court.
3. **Decision Date:** Exact date of the judgment order.
4. **Primary Judgment Text:** Verified PDF judgment from official court portals (e.g., ecourts.gov.in, sci.gov.in).

## 2. Incomplete Case Law Handling
Summaries or educational case narratives lacking the 4 items above are quarantined in `quarantine/incomplete-case-law/` and labeled as secondary material.
