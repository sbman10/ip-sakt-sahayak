description: Research and regulatory validation investigations
---

Use this workflow when exploring official sources, legal definitions, or technical implementation issues:

1. **Verify Primary Sources:**
   - Prioritize government databases (AYUSH portal, India Code, WIPO treaties).
   - Do not trust summaries from unofficial legal blogs.

2. **Summarize Legal Context:**
   - Clearly separate Indian laws (Rules, Sections, Acts) from international articles.

3. **Check Code Compatibility:**
   - Research library version alignments before introducing new packages.
