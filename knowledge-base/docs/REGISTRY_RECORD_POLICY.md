# Registry Record Policy

## 1. Scope
Registry records encompass:
- Granted patent specifications & journal notifications (Indian Patent Office / WIPO)
- Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL) references & non-patent literature prior art
- National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) Form I/II/III approvals & ABS determinations

## 2. Metadata Requirements
Every registry record must conform to `knowledge-base/schemas/registry-record.schema.yaml` and record:
- Canonical Record / Application Number
- Registration / Grant Date
- Current Status (Granted, Lapsed, Opposed, Under Review)
- Benefit sharing conditions and restrictions (if applicable)
