# Editorial & Knowledge Governance Policy

## 1. Hierarchy of Legal Sources
1. Primary Statutes (Parliamentary Acts)
2. Subordinate Rules & Statutory Regulations
3. Official Government Guidelines & Gazetted Notifications
4. Binding Judicial Precedents (Supreme Court & High Courts)
5. Authoritative Technical Formularies (API, IP, HPI Pharmacopoeias)
6. Secondary Commentary & Educational Summaries (Strictly segregated)

## 2. Handling Ambiguity & Conflicting Interpretations
When legal interpretations conflict between court divisions or regulatory bodies, both viewpoints must be documented with citations to their respective official decisions. No synthesis should present unresolved legal questions as definitive without noting the jurisdictional caveat.
